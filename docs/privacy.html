Pokédraw — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 13 September 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what data Pokédraw ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and how users can request deletion.

2. Data We Collect

  • Discord User IDs
  • Guild IDs
  • Channel IDs
  • Usernames and nicknames
  • Interactions such as slash commands, buttons, and menus
  • Message content, if the Message Content Intent is enabled
  • Card collection data, including cards owned, rarity, pack history, and trades

3. Why We Collect This Data

  • Managing card collections
  • Executing commands
  • Tracking pack openings, trades, and gameplay
  • Preventing abuse or exploitation
  • Improving features and user experience

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • Data is stored only as long as needed for Pokédraw's gameplay features.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Logs containing IDs or message content are purged regularly.
  • Users may request deletion of their data at any time.

5. Data Sharing

  • Discord, as required for normal bot operation
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers used to operate the Bot

6. User Rights

Users may request data deletion, data export, or clarification about stored data.

Email: pokezam@yahoo.com

7. Children's Privacy

Pokédraw follows Discord's minimum age requirement of 13.

8. Contact

Email: pokezam@yahoo.com
